⏱️ 1. Quick Setup Before the Competition Starts

1

Set Up Laptop A (Connected to the Projector)

Open /stage in Google Chrome on the laptop connected to the projector or TV screen.

Press F11 for Full Screen, and click the yellow Enable Sound button once at the top right.

2

Set Up Laptop B (The Admin / Controller Laptop)

Open /admin on the Quiz Master's or Tech Team's laptop.

Type password: AFCEKITI2026 and click Access Portal.

🎯 2. How to Run a Question (Step-by-Step)

1

Select the Team Answering

Under Live Stage, click on the Zone name (e.g. IDO, ADO, EMURE). Their box will light up in gold on the big screen.

2

Pick a Question Number

When the contestant picks a tile number (e.g., Tile 4), click on tile 4 on the Admin board. The scripture question opens instantly on the projector scroll.

3

Start the Participant Timer

As soon as the question is read, click ▶ Start. The circular countdown ring will appear on the projector screen.

Need extra time? Click +5s or +10s.

4

Judge the Answer

If the contestant answers correctly: Click ✔ CORRECT (+Points). The score increases and bell rings!

If they miss it: Click ✖ INCORRECT. The buzzer plays and the tile marks as answered.

5

Play Music / Applause

Use the DJ Soundboard at the bottom of the page to trigger real audio:

👏 Audience Clapping 🙌 Stadium Cheer 🎻 Mozart / Beethoven ⏹ Stop Audio

⚡ 3. How to Run Round 6 (Ultimate Challenge - 60s)

1

Select Round 6 & The Competing Zone

Switch to Round 6 (Ultimate Challenge). Click the Zone attempting the challenge.

2

Start the 60-Second Challenge

Click Start 60s Timer. The dramatic suspense heartbeat will play automatically.

3

Mark Answers Rapidly

As the contestant answers questions 1 through 10, click ✔ Correct or ✖ Pass/Wrong. The subtotal calculates in real-time (5 points per correct answer, up to 50 pts).

4

Commit the Score

When the 60 seconds are up, click Commit & Add Score to lock in their points on the official leaderboard.

🏆 4. Grand Podium Ceremony

★

Reveal the Final Winners

At the end of the competition, click 🏆 Reveal Grand Podium Ceremony in Admin.

The projector will trigger golden confetti, Beethoven's Ode to Joy fanfare, and reveal the 1st, 2nd, and 3rd Place Champions on the podium!

🛠️ 5. Quick Fixes & Troubleshooting

Issue Quick Solution
No sound on the projector Click the yellow "Enable Sound" button at the top right of the stage screen.
Accidental wrong score added Go to the "Zones Setup" tab in Admin → Click "Adjust Score" → Add or subtract points.
Contestant needs 5 more seconds Click the +5s or +10s blue button on the live timer control bar.
Stage screen not full screen Click on the stage browser window and press the F11 key on your keyboard.
💡 Golden Rule for the Quiz Controller: Always pick the Zone → Pick the Tile Number → Start Timer → Click Correct/Incorrect. That's it!
